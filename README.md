# Ranger Transition Master Plan

An interactive, browser-based transition checklist for members of the 75th Ranger Regiment working through their ETS timeline.

Built from the **Ranger Transition Blue Book** and structured around its ETS Transition Roadmap (SP → CP1 → CP2 → CP3 → CP4 → FP), it turns the roadmap into a working checklist you can fill in, check off, and track over the months leading up to separation.

## What it does

- **Phase-by-phase roadmap** — every task mapped to a checkpoint, working backwards from your ETS date.
- **The Transition Big 5** — Networking, Continuing Education/Employment, VA Integration & Health Care, Financial Literacy, Holistic Fitness — with a clear "what green looks like" for each.
- **Hard timing gates** — the deadlines that don't reopen (BDD claim window, TAP, VGLI, GI Bill transfer, dental, TRICARE) called out so they don't get missed.
- **CSP / SkillBridge approval-authority reference** — category, rank, days, and approval authority at a glance.
- **Fill-in fields and checkboxes** — enter your name, dates, and contacts; check items off as you complete them.
- **Progress saved locally** — your entries are cached in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Download My Copy** — export a snapshot with your entries locked in, to save or send to your mentor.

## How to use

Open the hosted page in your browser. Fill in your dates at the top, then work through the phases with your Three Rangers Foundation mentor. On mobile, use **Add to Home Screen** to run it like an app.

Your progress is private to your device. To move it between devices or share it with a mentor, use **Download My Copy**.

## Accuracy

Facts, dollar amounts, deadlines, and the CSP approval-authority table were cross-checked against the Ranger Transition Blue Book and AR 600-81. Installation-specific POCs (TAP offices, education counselors, Care Coalition) rotate — verify current contacts with your unit.
