SP Planning & Preparation — active duty, as early as possible

OBJECTIVE reduce the transition clutter
  • Take a Ranger Investment Planning assessment (via Three Rangers Foundation)
  • Confirm college credits with your Battalion Education Counselor — maximize service-earned credit before spending a dime
  • Register with ArmyIgnitED and Joint Special Operations University; map degree completion
  • Attend Ranger for Life symposiums; Chaplain Single Soldier events or Strong Bonds marriage retreats; consider an Operation Healing Forces retreat
  • Join the Global SOF Foundation — free while on active duty (SOF for Life job board)

CP1 Process — ETS−18 months

OBJECTIVE understand the tools + build your transition team
  • DoD-TAP pre-separation counseling (DD 2648) — schedule through your installation TAP office
  • Take the Initial Ranger Transition Checklist
  • Enroll in the A More Elite Transition course
  • Request a mentor from Three Rangers Foundation — this plan is built to be worked with that mentor
  • Apply to The Honor Foundation, SOTF, and/or the American Dream U Sprint Course
  • Determine income, health care, and insurance requirements for the family post-ETS
  • Verify USSOCOM Warrior Care Program (Care Coalition) eligibility if wounded, ill, or injured

CP2 Decision — ETS−12 to −10 months

OBJECTIVE gain confidence in your personal transition plan
  • Complete the 5-day DoD TAP + elective workshops (must attend within 12 months of ETS)
  • Make your fellowship decision: Army Career Skills Program (CSP/SkillBridge) or Care Coalition fellowship — see approval-authority table below and start command coordination early
  • Claim your one-year free LinkedIn Premium (active-duty program); build the profile
  • Request medical records from the installation hospital (not the Ranger clinic) for the initial VA disability claim — discs take weeks
  • Identify and select a Veterans Service Officer (VSO) for claims submission
  • Connect with Service to School and/or Military Assistance Foundation if pursuing a degree

CP3 Proficiency — ETS−10 to −6 months

OBJECTIVE school acceptance + bridge the military-to-civilian experience gap
  • Apply to Warrior-Scholar Project (LTWF reimburses travel for boot camps)
  • Apply to college / graduate school; apply for scholarships (RSF, National Ranger Foundation, LTWF CAP/GAP, Service to School list)
  • Attend a Hiring Our Heroes job fair
  • Stack continuing-education certification(s) — IVMF Onward to Opportunity, ACT Now Education, Credentialing Assistance ($2,000/FY, max 1 credential per FY, 3 over a 10-year span)
  • Build the master resume (STAR-format accomplishments, not a job description) and have five civilians in the target field review it

CP4 Rehearsal — ETS−6 months

OBJECTIVE experience the impacts of your transition decisions
  • Start the transition fellowship (CSP/SkillBridge or Care Coalition)
  • Submit the VA disability claim via BDD — 180–90 days before separation. Claims submitted after the window aren't processed until after separation.
  • Apply for VA Health Care (1010EZ); make the TRICARE plan decision; enroll BENEFEDS vision + dental
  • Apply for life insurance — ideally before the medical-records review and claim (insurability); VGLI window is 1 year + 120 days post-ETS
  • Final dental exam scheduled inside 180 days of separation
  • Review draft DD214 line by line — name, SSN, dates, awards, schools, RE code. The DD214 is yours — own it.

FP Ranger for Life — veteran

OBJECTIVE mentor the next generation of Ranger veterans
  • Visit your local state and federal VA Regional Office and a Vet Center; review all rating-based benefits
  • Join Wounded Warrior Project (if eligible) and confirm the VA decision letter with your VSO
  • Become a Ranger for Life mentor with Three Rangers Foundation — close the loop for the next man
  • Attend a local Ranger breakfast on the 1st Saturday of each month; join a military or civilian civic organization
  • Live the Ranger Creed.

! Hard timing gates — these windows do not reopen

  • BDD claim: ETS−180 to ETS−90. Miss it and the claim doesn't process until after separation — often a year-plus delay. Decision announced within ~30 days of separation when filed in window.
  • TAP 5-day workshop: within 12 months of ETS (mandatory registration through the installation office).
  • SkillBridge/CSP approval is the real gate, not the 180-day rule — Category I approvals run through the first field-grade commander; start paperwork 90–120 days before intended start.
  • Medical documentation: if it's not in the record before separation, it's hard to claim later. Every ailment, every visit — installation hospital records, not just the Ranger clinic.
  • Final dental exam: inside 180 days of separation.
  • TRICARE enrollment: 90 days post-separation (retirees) or coverage decisions lapse to space-available.
  • VGLI: apply within 1 year + 120 days of leaving — no-underwriting conversion of SGLI coverage.
  • GI Bill transfer to dependents carries a 4-year ADSO (active duty, Selected Reserve, or a combination) and must be initiated while still serving — no break in service, including time in the IRR.
  • Claims filed within one year of separation have higher success rates — chronicity is easier to establish.
